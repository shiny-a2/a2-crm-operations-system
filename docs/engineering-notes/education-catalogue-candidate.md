# Education catalogue and entry candidate

A locally tested source candidate replaces the short Education introduction with a fuller Persian product catalogue: clearer capabilities and availability, an interactive introduction to manager/consultant/student/reviewer workspaces, a demo guide, FAQ, mobile navigation and the existing A2 contact route.

The new login entry points visitors to the active isolated demonstration and explicitly explains that real-school account sign-in still needs implementation and activation. It does not collect credentials or create accounts. The catalogue identifies simulated guidance and unfinished capabilities rather than presenting them as live AI.

The interface retains Education book/calendar artwork, inherited Persian typography, the shared light/dark preference and A2 semantic colors. Additional folder/schedule illustrations support the workflow. Keyboard users can switch the role introduction, use the skip link, open/close FAQ items and close the mobile menu with Escape.

The optimized build and 14 existing synthetic regression/provenance tests passed. Four final mobile/desktop light/dark browser combinations produced 46 screenshots with no page exceptions or horizontal overflow; named text-palette contrast pairs measured at least 4.84:1. Checks included theme persistence after reload, menu/focus/disclosure states and the existing four-role local demo journey. This does not establish full accessibility, actual business login or complete product acceptance.

Status: version 0.1.1-demo.1 source candidate, not deployed. The published Education site remains on its earlier demonstration release. No real model request, external message/payment or production change occurred. Shared AI behavior, knowledge/memory and budgets remain owned by central A2 Intelligence.
