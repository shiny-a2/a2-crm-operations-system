# Education readiness review — 2026-11-05

The Education site runs the independent Node demonstration. A read-only check found its public introduction and demo entry available, while the standard product login route was still missing. Published guidance remains explicitly simulated. The current demonstration should not be described as a complete configurable CRM, management platform or student advisor.

The private implementation register now contains 90 stable tasks in 15 work areas. The original tasks retain their history; new slices cover the product catalogue and real login, governed business setup/settings, role administration, A2 visual consistency, connected guided demo journeys and shared-contract gaps. Each slice has an owner, dependencies and an acceptance condition.

The design direction keeps Education-specific book/calendar/conversation artwork while aligning typography, themes, forms, navigation, dashboards and interaction states with the newer A2 product language. This is a plan for further implementation, not a newly shipped visual redesign.

Chatbot and manager-agent behavior, knowledge/memory, model routing and AI cost/budget controls belong to the central A2 assistant platform. Products remain authorized business adapters and interfaces. Shared domain/identity/task contracts are reviewed with their existing owner rather than copied into another platform.

Public browser inspection covered 20 pages across Education and Beauty in eight product/theme/width combinations with no page exceptions or horizontal overflow. It used read-only requests; authenticated product workflows, complete accessibility and owner/expert acceptance were not revalidated. No paid model request, demo record creation or deployment occurred in this review.
